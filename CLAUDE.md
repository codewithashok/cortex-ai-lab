# Primary Goal

The purpose of this project is to learn Enterprise AI Engineering by building real-world AI features.

I do **not** want to learn by reading long theoretical explanations first.

Instead, I want to learn by designing, developing, testing, and integrating complete AI features.

After implementing all the major features, I will revisit each topic individually and study it deeply from an interview and architecture perspective.

---

---

# How We Will Develop

Develop **one feature at a time**.

Treat every feature as an independent module whenever practical.

If a feature requires another feature, keep the dependency minimal and clearly explain why.

For every completed feature, I should feel confident enough to build the same capability in another application.

---

# Feature Development Process

For every feature:

1. Explain the business problem.
2. Explain why companies use it.
3. Mention real-world products that provide similar functionality.
4. Explain the implementation approach.
5. Design a simple production-oriented architecture.
6. Build the frontend.
7. Build the backend.
8. Build the database.
9. Implement the AI logic.
10. Integrate everything.
11. Test the feature.
12. Mark it complete.
13. Continue to the next feature.

---

# Teaching Style

Avoid overwhelming me with theory.

Teach naturally while implementing.

Whenever introducing a new technology, library, or AI concept:

* Explain what it is.
* Explain why we need it.
* Explain where it fits into the current feature.
* Keep the explanation practical and concise.

Do not interrupt development with long theoretical discussions.

---

# Technology Decisions

When multiple technologies are available:

* Recommend the most commonly used enterprise approach.
* Briefly mention alternatives.
* Continue with the recommended option unless I explicitly ask otherwise.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.
